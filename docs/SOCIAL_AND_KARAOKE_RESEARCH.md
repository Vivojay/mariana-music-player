# Karaoke, sharing and account connections

Research reviewed 24 November 2026 (India). This extends the [original product and subscription comparison](MARIANA_COMPETITOR_RESEARCH.md). “Available” below describes Mariana source workflows. A store-approved integration or a licensed catalog has its own acceptance gate.

| Product | Capability used for comparison | Mariana implementation and remaining gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple Music Sing | Word highlighting, independent backing vocals, adjustable vocal level and a two-sided duet presentation for eligible tracks. [Apple](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2022/12/apple-introduces-apple-music-sing/) | Named concurrent singers, current/next phrases, a selectable part and a confidence gate. Native backing stems and portable isolated-vocal mixing have different prerequisites. Automatic catalog-wide alignments, licensed lyrics, translation and pronunciation coverage remain open. |
| Smule | People record performances and join vocal duets. [Publisher listing](https://apps.apple.com/in/app/smule-sing-karaoke-music/id509993510) | Listeners can exchange a small practice invitation selecting a singer and cue on an identically aligned recording. Split-screen recording, microphone latency calibration, pitch scoring, hosted performance matching and an invite-response vocal recording workflow are future work. |
| BandLab and Jamline | BandLab supports sharing projects for collaboration and forking. Jamline advertises recording, practice, groups and musician discovery. [BandLab](https://blog.bandlab.com/forking-and-collaboration-on-bandlab-explained/) · [Jamline](https://www.jamline.com/) | Session recipes, Matrix fan rooms, the clip composer and practice tracks cover portions of that workflow. Collaborative multitrack edits, take/version merging and low-latency network jamming remain open. |
| Spotify and TIDAL | Spotify Jam lets people add songs to a group session, with participation rules depending on plan and location. TIDAL shares music links across streaming services. [Spotify](https://support.spotify.com/au/article/jam/) · [TIDAL](https://support.tidal.com/hc/en-us/articles/23553629074193-Sharing-music-links-across-streaming-platforms) | Mariana can hand off public links and can control a single native session from a paired local browser. Authenticating an account does not start a Spotify Jam. Cross-service edition matching, multiuser queue permissions, entitlement checks and synchronized multi-device playback remain separate work. |
| YouTube / YouTube Music | YouTube now emphasizes sharing at a timestamp. [TeamYouTube](https://support.google.com/youtube/thread/425735532) | `clip link` creates a canonical watch URL with the selected time on an eligible YouTube recording. Private playlist or authentication fields are discarded. A link points to a start time. A rendered excerpt has both a start and an end. YouTube account identity and import are documented per scope in [Connections](CONNECTIONS.md). |
| Audacity and Audible India | Audacity edits selections and exports audio. Audible India distinguishes an in-app clip, a bookmark and the media a listener can share. [Audacity editing](https://support.audacityteam.org/basics/audacity-editing) · [Audacity export](https://support.audacityteam.org/basics/saving-and-exporting-projects) · [Audible India](https://help.audible.in/s/article/manage-clips-bookmarks?language=en_IN) | Mark in/out from a paused or playing source. Export a new audio/video file from eligible media without modifying the original. General multitrack production, protected-store audiobook extraction and paid catalog access are outside the delivered player. |
| EQUALS, Musicboard and Tunesimo | EQUALS centers on discussion and friendships. Musicboard includes ratings and lists. Tunesimo offers friend activity and weekly recaps. [EQUALS](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.equals.equals) · [Musicboard](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/musicboard-albums-songs/id1503544789) · [Tunesimo](https://help.tunesimo.com/en/articles/33-weekly-recap) | The music wall, shareable excerpts and fan rooms support sharing and discussion. Global discovery feeds, public reputation and moderation operations, ranked public lists, and private-by-default friend recaps are tracked in the [expansion status](PLAYER_EXPANSION_STATUS.md). |
| Bandcamp, Last.fm and ListenBrainz | Bandcamp has fan collections and direct artist support. Last.fm provides browser authorization and listening activity. ListenBrainz exposes public listening history. [Bandcamp](https://bandcamp.com/fans) · [Last.fm](https://www.last.fm/api/webauth) · [ListenBrainz](https://listenbrainz.readthedocs.io/en/latest/users/api/) | Profile links can open an artist or collection. Last.fm supports user authorization when the distributor registers Mariana. Users can request public listens for a ListenBrainz name. Merch checkout, private Bandcamp purchase access and automatic scrobbling are independent capabilities. |
| Resso | The service closed in India in January 2024. Its lyric-sharing experience provides a historical comparison. [Closure reporting](https://techcrunch.com/2024/01/11/bytedance-is-shutting-down-its-music-streaming-service-resso-in-india/) | Mariana's practice and sharing features work with user media. Resso account import or a live Resso integration is unavailable. |

## Social destinations

| Destination | What a user can do | Release checks or outstanding work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram and Facebook | Send supported files to installed share targets. In configured phone builds, prepare a compatible vertical clip for the Instagram Stories editor. The public-link composer can open Facebook’s share window. | The publisher supplies its Meta app ID for Stories. Test cancellation, both absent and installed apps, and real media acceptance per OS. Stories handoff lets the user complete the post. [Meta Stories](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/instagram-platform/sharing-to-stories) |
| Snapchat | Compatible Android targets can receive files. On the portable public-link page, selecting Snapchat loads Creative Kit Web in a sandboxed frame to share a public music URL. | Snap controls Snapcode generation and recipient availability. A desktop browser can restrict pop-ups or third-party scripts. Publishing to Spotlight and importing lenses require separate integrations. [Creative Kit](https://developers.snap.com/snap-kit/creative-kit/overview) · [Web setup](https://developers.snap.com/snap-kit/creative-kit/web) |
| TikTok | A compatible installed Android receiver can accept a file. Other supported hosts use the OS sheet or export. The user's own TikTok app performs the final edit and posting. | Login Kit, Share Kit and the Content Posting APIs solve distinct problems. Direct Post and server-side upload as a draft require registration, scopes and applicable review. Public posting, engagement counters and proof of upload are pending. [TikTok content publishing](https://developers.tiktok.com/docs/en/content-publishing-landing) |
| Other apps | The user chooses an installed receiver offered for that media type. Public links can also be copied or opened in WhatsApp. File download is available on supported browser export paths. | The receiving app decides which file types to accept. Feature detection and explicit user activation are required on browsers. A dismissed sheet is a cancellation. [Web Share API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Navigator/share) |

## Verification and release acceptance

Consult [karaoke](KARAOKE.md), [clip sharing](CLIP_SHARING.md), [portable clip formats](WEB_CLIPS.md), [account connections](CONNECTIONS.md) and [surface parity](SURFACE_PARITY.md) for commands, eligibility, setup and evidence. Automatic transcription or speaker attribution for arbitrary recordings, simultaneous remote performance, signed mobile store builds and audited social publishing require further work. A supplied alignment score is evidence about that timing source. It is not an independently calibrated probability that every sung word is correct.
